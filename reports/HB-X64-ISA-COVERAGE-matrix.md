## Remaining Intentionally Unsupported Semantics

- Full AVX/AVX2 is not claimed complete. `VMOVNT*`, `VLDDQU`, `VMOVNTDQA`, `VMOVHLPS/VMOVLHPS`, VEX.W ignored `VCMP*`/`VROUND*`, scalar FMA3 VEX.L ignored aliases, `VINSERTPS/VEXTRACTPS` WIG aliases, `VCOMI*/VUCOMI*`, `VPSHUF*`, `VMOV*DUP`, packed/scalar `VCVT*`, `VRCP*`, `VRSQRT*`, `VROUND*`, `VDPPS/VDPPD`, `VINSERTPS/VEXTRACTPS`, `VPINSR*/VPEXTR*`, `VMPSADBW`, `VGF2P8AFFINE*`, `VPERMIL*`, VEX `*BLENDV*`, `VCMP*`, `VUNPCK*`, `VSHUF*`, `VHADD*`, `VHSUB*`, `VADDSUB*`, FMA3 base/tail, VEX BMI1/BMI2 scalar execution, `VAESIMC`, `VMOVD/VMOVQ` reverse/store, movmask, `VTEST*`, `VMASKMOVDQU`, and MXCSR tails are covered in the current VEX evidence; current core VEX sweep over the Capstone-valid non-FMA/XOP corpus has no decode/lift failures.
- `PCMPxSTRx` now has focused byte/word mask/index/flag coverage, but broad string-compare mode coverage should be expanded before claiming exhaustive SSE4.2.
- EVEX/AVX-512 is modeled only for the sampled smoke families listed above, including `k1` merge and `k1{z}` zeroing where recorded plus sampled compare-to-k writes. Broader opmask-control tails, broad SAE/rounding, and broad AVX-512 semantics remain unsupported.
- FMA3 and F16C have focused interpreter regression coverage, but this Unicorn 2.1.4 build returns `UC_ERR_INSN_INVALID` for those VEX opcodes, so they are not claimed as Unicorn-executed oracle cases.
- `xbegin` decodes to controlled unsupported system semantics; TSX execution is not modeled.
- Privileged/control/debug register MOV samples decode and lift to controlled unsupported/fault behavior; they are not executed as user-mode success.
